[[法语语音笔记.pdf#page=4|View original PDF · Page 4]]

### Asking Someone's Name

> [!example] Comment tu t'appelles ?
> **Comment** (`gu + mang`) **tu** (`dyu`) **t'** (`da`) **appelles** (`belle`) ?  
> **[What is your name?]**  
> 你叫什么？

> [!example] Je m'appelle...
> **[My name is...]**  
> 我叫……

### Forms of Address

| French              | English          | 中文    | Pronunciation cue  |
| ------------------- | ---------------- | ----- | ------------------ |
| **monsieur MACRON** | [Mr. Macron]     | 马克龙先生 | hong near *MACRON* |
| **madame ZHANG**    | [Ms./Mrs. Zhang] | 张女士   |                    |
| **mademoiselle**    | [Miss]           | 小姐    |                    |

> [!tip] Handwritten Sound Observations
> - `t → d`
> - `p → b`
> - `k → g`
> - `s → z` 两个元音之间
> - `ou → u`
